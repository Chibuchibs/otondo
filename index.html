    About Otondo

    Otondo is a free Lagos street game you play in your browser. No download, no sign up. You walk the road as a corps member: dodge Mr. Pothole, step round the broken-down danfo, buy pure water and akara from the hawkers, catch the thief and quench the fire.

    The street changes as you walk: Allen Avenue in Ikeja, Broad Street on Lagos Island, Admiralty Way in Lekki Phase 1, Computer Village in Ikeja and Ojuelegba in Yaba, each with its own buildings, skyline and sellers.

    Underneath it is 21 days of NYSC camp. You arrive with ₦20,000 and allawee never enter: parade, Man O War, Mami market and bad beans. Build your credibility, keep your belle full, make the money last, then find out where dem post you. In camp, an otondo is the fresh corper who doesn't know how things work yet. Everybody starts as one.
